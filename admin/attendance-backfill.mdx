---
title: "Attendance backfill"
description: "Correct or add past attendance records when entries were missed."
---

Sometimes a sign-in or sign-out gets missed — a parent forgets to scan, or a staff member skips the kiosk. Attendance backfill lets you correct these gaps so your records stay accurate for compliance.

<img src="/images/admin/attendance-overview.png" alt="Attendance log with date navigation" />

## Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the attendance log">
    Navigate to **Attendance Log** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to the correct date">
    Use the date picker or arrow buttons to go to the day that needs correction. You can also click **Yesterday** for quick access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or correct the entry">
    Find the child or staff member in the list and add the missing sign-in or sign-out time. The record updates immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

The attendance log shows daily records for both children and staff, switchable via the **Children** and **Staff** tabs at the top. You can view records in three modes:

| View | Shows |
|------|-------|
| **Today** | Current day with live auto-refresh every 60 seconds |
| **This Week** | Monday through Friday of the selected week |
| **This Month** | Full month view |

When viewing a past date, you can add entries that were missed. Each correction is logged with a timestamp so you maintain an audit trail.

## Exporting records

After correcting records, you can export the day's attendance as a CSV file using the **CSV** button. Use the **Print** button to generate a printable version for your physical records.

<Tip>Review yesterday's attendance each morning as part of your daily routine. Catching missed entries early keeps your records clean for inspections.</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="Cannot find a child in the attendance log">
  The attendance log only shows children who are on your active roster. If a child is missing, check the Child Roster page to confirm their enrollment status is active.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Export button is disabled">
  CSV export is only available in the **Today** view mode. Switch to Today view and select the date you want to export.
</Accordion>
