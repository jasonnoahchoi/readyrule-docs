Your Facility Intel report analyzes inspection patterns, regional activity, and risk factors to give you an early warning system for upcoming inspections.

<img src="/images/admin/facility-intel-overview.png" alt="Facility intelligence page showing risk factors table" />

## Reading your report

Navigate to **Facility Intel** in the sidebar under Intelligence. Your report shows:

- **Days since last visit** — how long since your last inspection
- **Similar facilities** — how many comparable facilities are tracked
- **Risk level** — overall assessment with active factor count
- **6-factor table** — each risk factor with status and details

### Risk levels

| Level | What it means |
|-------|---------------|
| **All clear** | Low risk. Keep doing what you're doing. |
| **Monitor** | Some factors need attention but nothing urgent. |
| **Elevated** | Multiple risk factors active. Review recommendations. |
| **Urgent** | High risk of inspection. Take action this week. |

## The 6 risk factors

Your report tracks these factors that predict inspection likelihood:

<Accordion title="Days overdue">
How long since your last inspection compared to similar facilities. When **ACTIVE**, you're overdue compared to most facilities — expect an inspection soon.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Hot zone">
Whether inspectors have been visiting nearby facilities recently. When **ACTIVE**, inspectors visited facilities near you in the last 14 days — your turn may be coming.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Citation pattern">
Your recent citation history. **ACTIVE** means citations in the last 90 days. Review what was cited and ensure corrections are documented.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Cluster risk">
How many overdue facilities are near you. Regional offices often schedule sweep visits when multiple facilities in an area are overdue.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="RO activity spike">
Whether your regional office is conducting more inspections than usual. This often happens at quarter-end or when new inspectors are assigned.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Complaint flag">
Whether complaints have been filed against your facility in the last 180 days. Complaints can trigger priority inspections.
</Accordion>

## Taking action

Below the risk factors, review the **Recommended Actions** section. Each action has a priority:

- **Urgent** — do this today
- **High** — schedule this week
- **Medium** — plan within 1-2 weeks
- **Low** — address when convenient

<Tip>Check your report weekly — it updates every Monday morning. Screenshot it for staff meetings to show your proactive approach.</Tip>
