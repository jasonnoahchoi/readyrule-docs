ReadyRule keeps attendance records for children and staff for 3 years, meeting California Title 22 requirements. You can export these records as CSV files for inspections and view daily staff attendance history in the Attendance Log.

<img src="/images/admin/attendance-export-overview.png" alt="Attendance export page with date range and record type selectors" />

## Export records for an inspection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Attendance Reports">
    Navigate to **Attendance Reports** from the sidebar. You will see a page with multiple report tabs across the top.
    <img src="/images/admin/attendance-reports-overview.png" alt="Attendance Reports page with tab navigation" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Export Records tab">
    Click **Export Records** (the last tab in the row). You will see a date range picker, record type selector, and a Download button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your date range">
    Pick a **From** date and a **To** date. The default is last month to today.

    <Tip>The maximum export range is 1 year per download. For longer periods, run multiple exports with different date ranges.</Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the record type">
    | Option | What's included |
    |--------|----------------|
    | **Child Attendance** | Date, child name, age group, sign-in/out times, duration, adult who signed |
    | **Staff Attendance** | Date, staff name, role, sign-in/out times, total break hours, net hours worked |
    | **Both** | Downloads two separate CSV files (one for children, one for staff) |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Download CSV">
    The file downloads immediately. If you chose "Both," you get two files:
    - `attendance-children-2026-01-01-to-2026-03-01.csv`
    - `staff-attendance-2026-01-01-to-2026-03-01.csv`

    If no records exist for that range, you will see "No attendance records found for this date range."
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View staff attendance history

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Attendance Log">
    Navigate to **Attendance Log** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Staff tab">
    At the top, you will see two tabs: **Children** and **Staff**. Click **Staff**. You will see a list of staff members with their events for today, grouped by person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse a staff member's day">
    Each staff member shows their events in order:

    ```text
    Jane Smith (Director)
      8:02 AM   Signed In
      12:00 PM  Started Break
      12:30 PM  Ended Break
      4:01 PM   Signed Out
      Net hours: 7h 29m
    ```

    The summary line at the top shows quick counts: "3 staff - 1 on site - 1 on break - 1 signed out"
  </Step>
  <Step title="View past dates">
    Use the **date picker** to navigate to any past date. Staff attendance history is available for the full 3-year retention window.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understanding the data

### How net hours are calculated

Net hours = total time on site minus break time.

| Staff status | Hours shown |
|-------------|-------------|
| Signed out for the day | Final net hours (exact) |
| Still on site | Estimated hours with "(in progress)" — updates live |
| On break | Total time on site so far (break duration not yet subtracted) |

### Event types

| Icon | Action | Meaning |
|------|--------|---------|
| Green check | Signed In | Staff arrived at the facility |
| Red door | Signed Out | Staff left for the day |
| Yellow coffee | Started Break | Staff began a break |
| Blue play | Ended Break | Staff returned from break |

## Data retention policy

ReadyRule stores attendance records for **3 years** from the date of the event, per California Title 22 requirements.

- Records are available for export at any time within this window
- If your facility cancels its subscription, you have 90 days to export your data
- Data is backed up daily on our servers

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="The export is taking a long time">
  Large date ranges (close to 1 year) with many children and staff can take a few seconds. Wait for the download to complete. If it fails, try a shorter date range.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The CSV looks wrong when I open it in Excel">
  CSV files use commas as delimiters. If Excel doesn't parse it correctly:
  1. Open Excel
  2. Go to **Data > From Text/CSV**
  3. Select the downloaded file
  4. Choose "Comma" as the delimiter
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I don't see the Export Records tab">
  Only admins and owners can access the export feature. If you are a staff member, ask your admin.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A staff member's hours look wrong">
  Check their event history in Attendance Log > Staff tab for that date. Common causes:
  - They forgot to clock out (shows "in progress" indefinitely)
  - A break was started but never ended
  - Events were manually edited — check the audit trail
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I export PDF reports?">
  PDF export is planned but not yet available. Currently, CSV is the supported format.
</Accordion>
