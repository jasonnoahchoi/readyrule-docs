Regional office intelligence aggregates public CCLD data into a profile for each regional licensing office, giving you visibility into the inspection patterns that affect your facility.

<img src="/images/admin/regional-office-intelligence-overview.png" alt="Regional office intelligence page showing office profile, rankings, and visit trends" />

## Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your regional office page">
    Navigate to **Intel** from the sidebar, then select your regional office. ReadyRule automatically highlights the office assigned to your facility.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review performance rankings">
    Scroll to the rankings card to see how your office compares statewide on inspection coverage, complaint volume, and citation rate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare other offices">
    Use the office selector dropdown at the top to switch between any regional office in California.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

Each regional office page displays four sections built from 12 weeks of aggregated data:

**Office profile** — Address, phone number, and the counties the office serves.

**Performance rankings** — Key metrics including total facilities, inspection coverage percentage, complaint visit rate, and citations per facility. Each metric includes a statewide rank badge (green for top 5, orange for top 10, red for 11+).

**Visit activity** — An area chart showing weekly visit volume over the past 12 weeks, with a breakdown by visit type (annual, complaint, follow-up).

**Citation analysis** — A weekly trend chart for citations alongside a bar chart breaking down citations by type (Type A, Type B, etc.).

<Tip>Check your regional office page before inspection season. Offices with high inspection coverage rates and rising visit trends are actively working through their caseloads.</Tip>
