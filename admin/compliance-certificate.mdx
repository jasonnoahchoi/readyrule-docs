The compliance certificate is a printable document summarizing your facility's attendance and ratio compliance over a date range. Use it during licensing audits or to share compliance status with parents.

## Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open attendance reports">
    Navigate to **Attendance Reports** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a date range">
    Switch to the **Range** report type. Set your desired start and end dates using the date pickers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and print">
    The compliance certificate appears below the range summary. Click **Print Certificate** to open your browser's print dialog. The certificate is formatted for clean printing with non-essential UI elements hidden automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

The certificate is generated from your attendance records and displays:

- **Facility information** — name, license number, facility type, and reporting period
- **Compliance status** — a prominent **COMPLIANT** or **REVIEW RECOMMENDED** badge based on your metrics
- **Compliance metrics** — total operating days, average daily attendance, compliance rate, ratio violations, total check-ins, and override rate

A facility is marked **COMPLIANT** when the compliance rate is 95% or higher and there are zero ratio violations. Otherwise, the certificate shows **REVIEW RECOMMENDED** with details on which metrics need attention.

Each certificate includes a unique document ID and generation timestamp for audit trail purposes.

<Tip>Generate a certificate at the end of each month and save the PDF for your records. This builds a compliance history that is useful during annual inspections.</Tip>

<Warning>The compliance rate currently reflects attendance override rates. Staff-to-child ratio tracking will be incorporated in a future update when real-time ratio data is available.</Warning>
