ReadyRule walks you through a guided setup the first time you log in. Within a few minutes, you can have your facility fully configured and ready for daily compliance tracking.

<img src="/images/admin/getting-started-dashboard.png" alt="ReadyRule dashboard after first login" />

## Product tour

When you first visit your dashboard, a guided tour highlights the key areas:

1. **Compliance score** — your live compliance rating based on citation history
2. **Active children** — current roster count with attendance tracking
3. **Staff count** — team overview and certification status
4. **Alerts** — citation deadlines, follow-ups, and action items
5. **AI chat** — ask questions about compliance or regulations
6. **Settings** — configure notifications and manage your account

Use **Next** to advance through each step, or close the tour at any time.

### Replay the tour

To see the tour again:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Select the gear icon in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Help and Support">
    Scroll to the **Help & Support** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Replay Tour">
    You will return to the dashboard and the tour restarts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Setup checklist

After the tour, a **Getting Started** checklist appears on your dashboard. It tracks four setup steps:

| Step | What to do | Where |
|------|------------|-------|
| Add your staff members | Enter staff names, roles, and certifications | Staff page |
| Import your child roster | Upload a CSV or add children manually | Child Roster page |
| Review your citation history | Verify your facility's citations are loaded | Citations page |
| Configure notification preferences | Set up email and push alerts for deadlines | Settings page |

Completed items show a green checkmark. A progress bar tracks your overall completion. Click any item to go directly to that page. The checklist disappears automatically when all items are done, or dismiss it early with the **X** button.

<img src="/images/admin/getting-started-sidebar.png" alt="Sidebar navigation after setup" />

## What to do next

After completing the checklist:

- Check your **dashboard** daily for compliance alerts and action items
- Use **AI chat** to ask questions about California childcare regulations
- Review **citations** and track plan-of-correction deadlines
- Monitor **staff certifications** and training requirements

<Tip>Bookmark your dashboard for quick daily access. The compliance score updates in real time as you resolve alerts and complete tasks.</Tip>
