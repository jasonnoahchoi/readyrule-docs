---
title: "Custom sign-in URL"
description: "Set up a custom vanity URL for your facility's parent sign-in page."
---

By default, parents access your sign-in page using your facility number (e.g., `readyrule.com/go/013423682`). A custom sign-in URL replaces that number with a human-readable name like `readyrule.com/go/sunshine-daycare`.

<img src="/images/admin/vanity-url-settings.png" alt="Custom sign-in URL setting in Settings" />

## Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the custom sign-in URL setting">
    Under **Facility Profile**, locate the custom sign-in URL field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your preferred slug">
    Type a short, memorable name for your facility. The field auto-formats to lowercase and strips invalid characters. A live preview shows the full URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. ReadyRule checks that the name is available before confirming.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## URL format rules

Your custom slug must follow these rules:

- 4 to 32 characters long
- Lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only
- No spaces or special characters

ReadyRule validates the format as you type and checks availability when you leave the field.

## Changing your URL

You can change your custom URL, but there is a **30-day cooldown** between changes. The settings page shows when you can next update the slug. A confirmation dialog appears before overwriting an existing slug.

Both the old numeric URL and the new custom URL work for parent sign-in. Updating the slug does not break existing QR codes that use your facility number.

<Tip>Choose a slug that matches how parents know your facility — your business name or a common abbreviation. Keep it short so it is easy to share verbally.</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="Slug is already taken">
  Another facility has claimed that name. Try adding a location identifier, like `sunshine-daycare-oakland`.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Cannot change URL yet">
  The 30-day cooldown is active. The settings page shows the exact date when you can make another change.
</Accordion>
