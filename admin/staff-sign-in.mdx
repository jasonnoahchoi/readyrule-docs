Staff clock-in lets your team record when they arrive, leave, and take breaks. You can use QR codes, a front desk kiosk, or both.

<img src="/images/admin/staff-sign-in-overview.png" alt="Staff sign-in overview showing QR and kiosk options" />

## Set up the QR code

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings** from the sidebar (gear icon at the bottom). You will see a "QR Codes" section with two QR codes — one for parents and one for staff.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the staff sign-in QR code">
    Scroll down to the **Staff Sign-In** section. You will see a QR code and a direct link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print or share the QR code">
    You have three options:

    | Action | What it does |
    |--------|-------------|
    | **Copy Link** | Copies the sign-in URL to your clipboard — share via text or email |
    | **Download QR** | Saves the QR code as an image file |
    | **Print for Wall** | Opens a print-ready page with the QR code and instructions |

    <Tip>Print the QR code and post it near your entrance at eye level. Staff can scan it from a few feet away.</Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set up the front desk kiosk

If you have a shared tablet or laptop at your front desk, you can set up a kiosk page where staff pick their name and enter a PIN.

<img src="/images/admin/staff-sign-in-kiosk.png" alt="Staff kiosk sign-in screen with name list" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the kiosk URL">
    In the Staff Sign-In QR section, look for the **Kiosk / Front Desk Setup** box. It shows a URL like:

    ```text
    https://readyrule.com/staff-kiosk/your-facility
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bookmark it on your tablet">
    Open that URL in your tablet's browser and add it to your home screen or bookmarks. You will see a list of all active staff names. No login is needed on the tablet itself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set PINs for each staff member">
    Staff need a 4-digit PIN to use the kiosk. You set these in staff settings:

    1. Go to **Staff** in the sidebar
    2. Click on a staff member's name
    3. Scroll down to the **Attendance PIN** section
    4. Click **Set PIN**
    5. Enter a 4-digit number and confirm it
    6. Click **Save PIN**

    Repeat for each staff member who will use the kiosk.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing PINs

### Change a staff member's PIN

1. Go to **Staff** and click the staff member
2. In the **Attendance PIN** section, click **Change PIN**
3. Enter the new 4-digit PIN and confirm
4. Click **Save PIN**

### Remove a PIN

1. Go to **Staff** and click the staff member
2. Click **Change PIN**, then click **Remove**

The staff member will no longer be able to use the kiosk until you set a new PIN.

## How staff use it

### QR code (personal phone)

1. Staff scans the QR code with their phone camera
2. They log into ReadyRule if they are not already logged in
3. They see their current status and tap **Clock In**, **Clock Out**, **Start Break**, or **Break Over**

### Kiosk (shared tablet)

1. Staff taps their name from the list
2. They enter their 4-digit PIN
3. They see their status and tap the action they need
4. A confirmation appears, then it auto-returns to the name list for the next person

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="A staff member doesn't see their name on the kiosk">
  They must be marked as **active** in ReadyRule. Go to **Staff** and check their status.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The QR code takes staff to a login page">
  That is expected. The QR code flow requires each staff member to be logged into ReadyRule on their own phone. If they don't have an account, invite them first.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can staff set their own PIN?">
  Yes. On the QR code page (personal phone), if they are logged in and don't have a PIN yet, they will see an option to set one themselves.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if someone enters the wrong PIN too many times?">
  The system locks them out for 5 minutes after 3 wrong attempts. This prevents guessing. Wait 5 minutes and try again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I see who clocked in today?">
  Yes. The attendance records appear in **Attendance** > daily view. Each event shows the time, action, and method (QR code or manual).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A staff member's name shows up but they can't clock in">
  They don't have a PIN set yet. Go to their staff profile and set one.
</Accordion>
