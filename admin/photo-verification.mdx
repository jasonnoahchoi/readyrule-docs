title: "Photo verification"

---

Photo verification adds a visual identity check during child pickup. Staff verify the adult's photo before completing the handoff, reducing unauthorized pickup risk.

<img src="/images/admin/photo-verification-settings.png" alt="Photo verification toggle in settings" />

## Setting up photo verification

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open settings">
    Go to **Settings** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find photo verification">
    Scroll to the **Photo Verification** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle on">
    Enable the toggle. When on, staff must verify the adult's photo before completing pickup. When off, pickup proceeds without the photo step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Enable photo verification once at least 50% of authorized adults have photos on file.</Tip>

## How it works

When photo verification is enabled:

1. An authorized adult arrives for pickup
2. Staff open the attendance screen and select the child
3. The adult's photo appears for visual confirmation
4. Staff verify the person matches the photo
5. Pickup is completed

## Photo coverage

Track your facility's photo coverage to measure adoption:

| Coverage | Status | Action |
|----------|--------|--------|
| 70%+ | Excellent | Maintain current coverage |
| 50-69% | Good | Continue encouraging uploads |
| Below 50% | Needs attention | Prioritize photo collection |

## Collecting photos

Photos can be added by staff during registration or at any time from the child's profile. Requirements:

- Format: JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, or HEIC
- Size: Under 5MB
- Content: Clear, recent face photo (well-lit, full face visible)

## Privacy

- Photos are stored with encryption and only visible to authenticated staff
- Signed URLs expire after 1 hour
- Parents can request photo removal at any time
- Photo collection is optional

<Accordion title="Handling parent concerns">
**"Why do you need my photo?"** — Photos help staff verify identity quickly during pickup, keeping your child safe.

**"Who can see it?"** — Only staff at your facility. Photos are stored securely and not shared externally.

**"Can I refuse?"** — Yes, photos are optional. Phone verification continues as the fallback method.
</Accordion>
