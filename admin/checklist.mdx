The daily checklist gives you peace of mind in 30 seconds. It surfaces 4-6 compliance items personalized to your facility type, so you can verify everything is in order before the day begins.

<img src="/images/admin/checklist-overview.png" alt="Daily compliance checklist" />

## Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the checklist">
    Navigate to **Checklist** in the sidebar, or tap the checklist quick action on the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review each item">
    The checklist shows items relevant to your facility type and subtype. Read each item and confirm it is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark items done">
    Check off each item as you verify it. Your progress is tracked for the day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Two modes

The checklist supports two modes:

| Mode | Purpose | Items |
|------|---------|-------|
| **Daily** | Quick daily verification | 4-6 personalized items |
| **Prep** | Pre-inspection preparation | All compliance items |

The default mode is **daily**. Switch to **prep** mode when you want a comprehensive review before an expected inspection.

## How it works

Each checklist item is tailored to your facility's license type and operational characteristics. Items cover areas like:

- Staff-to-child ratio verification
- Required postings and documentation
- Health and safety checks
- Emergency preparedness

The checklist resets each day so you start fresh every morning.

<Tip>Make the checklist part of your opening routine. Completing it daily builds a habit that keeps your facility inspection-ready at all times.</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="Checklist items do not match my facility type">
  The checklist personalizes items based on your facility profile. If items seem incorrect, verify your facility type in Settings or contact support.
</Accordion>
