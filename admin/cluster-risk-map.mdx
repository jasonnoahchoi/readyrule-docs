The cluster risk map shows nearby childcare facilities on an interactive map, color-coded by inspection status. Use it to understand how much inspector activity is concentrated in your area and anticipate when visits may increase.

<img src="/images/admin/cluster-risk-map-overview.png" alt="Cluster risk map showing nearby facilities with color-coded markers" />

## Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the cluster risk map">
    Navigate to **Cluster Risk** from the sidebar or command center. The map loads automatically based on your facility's coordinates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your risk level">
    Check the risk badge in the map header. It displays **High**, **Moderate**, or **Low** based on how many nearby facilities are overdue for inspection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore nearby facilities">
    Click any marker on the map to see facility details, including their last visit date and inspection status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

ReadyRule plots all licensed facilities within a 3-mile radius of your location on an interactive map. Each marker is color-coded:

- **Purple** — your facility
- **Red** — overdue for inspection
- **Orange** — due soon
- **Blue** — recently visited
- **Green** — compliant

The cluster risk level is calculated from the number of overdue facilities nearby:

| Overdue count | Risk level |
|---|---|
| 5 or more | High |
| 2 to 4 | Moderate |
| 0 to 1 | Low |

When multiple facilities in your area are overdue, inspectors often schedule visits in clusters to cover the region efficiently. A high cluster risk means your area is more likely to receive inspector attention soon.

<Tip>If your area has fewer than 3 nearby facilities, ReadyRule shows a summary card instead of the full map. This means you are in a sparse area with naturally lower cluster risk.</Tip>

<Warning>The cluster risk map requires facility coordinates from CCLD data. If your facility's coordinates are unavailable, the map will display an informational message instead.</Warning>
