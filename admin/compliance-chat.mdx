Compliance Chat is your AI-powered assistant for California childcare regulations. It can answer questions about Title 22 requirements, help you prepare for licensing inspections, draft Plans of Correction for citations, explain staff-to-child ratio requirements, and guide you through fire drill and safety documentation.

<img src="/images/admin/compliance-chat-interface.png" alt="Compliance Chat interface with conversation sidebar and message area" />

<Tip>A disclaimer at the bottom of every chat reminds you that AI guidance is not legal advice. Always verify with your Licensing Program Analyst (LPA) for official determinations.</Tip>

## Quick start: ask your first question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Compliance Chat">
    From anywhere in ReadyRule, click **Ask AI** in the left navigation menu. You will see a personalized welcome with your name and facility. Your conversation history appears in the left sidebar, organized by date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new conversation">
    Click the purple **[+]** button next to "Conversations" to start fresh, or click **"Start New Conversation"** in the main area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your question">
    In the text box at the bottom, type your question and press Enter or click the send button.

    Example questions to try:
    - "What are the staff-to-child ratios for infants?"
    - "How should I prepare for my upcoming inspection?"
    - "What are the fire drill requirements for my facility?"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the response">
    The AI provides formatted answers that may include bold headings, numbered lists, specific regulation citations (e.g., "Per Section 101216.3(a)..."), actionable steps, and suggestions for follow-up questions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Core tasks

### Preparing for inspections

Ask: "How should I prepare for an upcoming inspection?"

The AI will provide a checklist tailored to your facility type, including documentation to have ready, common citation areas to review, staff training reminders, and physical environment checks.

<Tip>Ask this question 2-3 weeks before your expected inspection window.</Tip>

### Understanding citations

Ask: "Help me understand [paste your citation text]"

The AI will explain what the citation means, why it matters, and what evidence of correction inspectors typically want.

### Drafting Plans of Correction

Ask: "Draft a Plan of Correction for [describe the citation]"

The AI generates a draft POC with acknowledgment of the issue, immediate corrective actions, ongoing prevention measures, and target completion dates.

For better results, provide specific details. For example: "Draft a POC for a staff ratio violation that occurred during naptime when one teacher was on break."

### Staff ratio questions

Ask: "What are the required staff-to-child ratios for [age group]?" or "If I have 3 infants and 8 toddlers, how many staff do I need?"

The AI will calculate based on California Title 22 requirements and your facility's license type.

### Fire drill and safety requirements

Ask: "What are the fire drill requirements for my facility?" or "How often do I need to conduct disaster drills?"

The AI will explain required drill frequency, documentation requirements, and what inspectors look for.

## Quick actions and follow-ups

### Quick action pills

When you start a new conversation, you will see quick action buttons below the welcome message:

- **Fire drill rules** — Jump to fire safety requirements
- **CPR renewal** — Check certification expiration dates
- **Staff ratios** — Get ratio requirements for your facility type
- **Inspection prep** — Start an inspection preparation checklist
- **What's due?** — See upcoming compliance deadlines

Click any pill to ask that question without typing.

### Suggested follow-ups

After the AI responds, you may see follow-up question pills at the bottom of the response. Click any to continue the conversation.

### Action buttons in responses

Some AI responses include action buttons that link to relevant features:

- **"Log Fire Drill Now"** — Takes you to the fire drill logging page
- **"View Requirements"** — Opens the relevant regulation details
- **"Create action plan"** — Starts generating an action plan from the response

## Sharing and printing responses

When you hover over an AI response, a share icon appears. Click it to:

| Option | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| **Copy** | Copies the response text to your clipboard |
| **Email** | Opens your email client with the response pre-filled |
| **Print** | Opens a print-friendly view of the response |

<Tip>Print a response about fire drill requirements to post in your staff room, or email an inspection prep checklist to your team.</Tip>

## Managing conversations

### Finding past conversations

Your previous chats are listed in the left sidebar, organized by Today, Yesterday, This Week, and Older. Use the search box to find conversations by keyword.

### Archiving a conversation

Hover over a conversation in the list, click the menu, then select **Archive**. Check **"Show archived"** to see conversations you've archived.

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="The AI gave me incorrect information">
  AI can make mistakes. If something seems wrong:
  1. Ask a follow-up question for clarification
  2. Cross-reference with your LPA or official Title 22 documentation
  3. The AI's knowledge is based on regulations as of its training date
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I can't find my previous conversation">
  1. Use the search box in the sidebar
  2. Check "Show archived" in case it was archived
  3. Conversations are sorted by most recent activity
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The response is taking a long time">
  Large or complex questions may take 10-30 seconds. If it seems stuck, wait up to 30 seconds, refresh the page if needed, or start a new conversation.
</Accordion>

## Best practices

1. **Be specific** — "What are the ratios for infants in a large family childcare home?" is better than "What are the ratios?"
2. **Provide context** — When asking about citations or POCs, include details about your situation.
3. **Ask follow-ups** — If the first answer is not complete, ask clarifying questions in the same conversation.
4. **Save responses** — Copy and save responses you will need to reference later.
5. **Verify decisions** — Always confirm critical compliance decisions with your LPA.
