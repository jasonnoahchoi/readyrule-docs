The alerts page is your central inbox for compliance notifications. It shows citation deadlines, follow-ups, certification expirations, and other action items that need your attention.

<img src="/images/admin/alerts-overview.png" alt="Alerts page showing grouped notifications" />

## Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open alerts">
    Navigate to **Alerts** in the sidebar, or tap the alert count badge on the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review grouped alerts">
    Alerts are grouped by date: **Today**, **This Week**, and **Earlier**. Unread alerts are highlighted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take action">
    Click an alert to see details and resolve it. Mark individual alerts as read, or use **Mark All Read** to clear the unread count.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Alert types

ReadyRule generates alerts for several compliance events:

| Alert type | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Citation deadlines** | Plan-of-correction due dates approaching or overdue |
| **Certification expiring** | Staff certifications expiring within 30 days |
| **Background check** | Clearance status changes or pending items |
| **Attendance gaps** | Missing sign-in or sign-out records |
| **Ratio warnings** | Staff-to-child ratio approaching limits |

Each alert shows its priority level. Urgent alerts appear at the top of the dashboard and in the stat chips row.

## Managing alerts

- **Mark as read** — click an individual alert to update its status
- **Mark all read** — use the bulk action to clear all unread notifications
- **SMS notification** — some alerts support sending an SMS reminder directly from the alert
- **Resolve** — follow the alert's link to the relevant page and complete the required action

The unread count appears in the page header so you always know how many items need attention.

<Tip>Check your alerts page at the start of each day. Resolving alerts promptly keeps your compliance score high and prevents deadline surprises.</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="Alerts are not appearing">
  Verify your notification preferences in Settings. Some alert types can be toggled on or off based on your preferences.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Cannot dismiss an alert">
  Some alerts require action before they can be resolved. Follow the link in the alert to complete the necessary step.
</Accordion>
