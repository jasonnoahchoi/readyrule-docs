Compliance Chat is an AI assistant that can answer your questions about childcare regulations. Use it when you need quick answers about staff-to-child ratios, what to do in specific situations, documentation requirements, or safety and emergency procedures.

<img src="/images/staff/compliance-chat-overview.png" alt="Compliance Chat interface for staff members" />

<Tip>A disclaimer at the bottom of every chat reminds you that the AI provides guidance, not official rulings. When in doubt, check with your director.</Tip>

## Quick start: get an answer fast

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the chat">
    Click **"Ask AI"** in the menu on the left side of the screen. You will see a personalized welcome with your name and facility.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your question">
    At the bottom of the screen, type what you want to know and press Enter.

    Example questions:
    - "How many toddlers can I have in my room with 2 staff?"
    - "What should I do if a parent is late for pickup?"
    - "How do I document an incident?"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the answer">
    The AI responds with a formatted answer that may include bold headings, numbered steps, the specific rule that applies, and suggestions for follow-up questions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions to ask

### Ratios

Ask: "How many [age group] children can I supervise alone?"

Example: "How many preschoolers can I have with just me in the room?"

The AI will tell you the maximum number based on California rules.

### Incident documentation

Ask: "How do I document [type of incident]?"

Example: "How do I document a child's minor injury?"

The AI will explain what to write down, who needs to be notified, and where to file the report.

### Emergency procedures

Ask: "What do I do if [emergency situation]?"

Example: "What do I do if a child has an allergic reaction?"

The AI gives you step-by-step guidance. For actual emergencies, always follow your facility's emergency procedures first and call 911 if needed.

## Tips for getting good answers

| Instead of... | Try... |
|---------------|--------|
| "What are the ratios?" | "What's the ratio for 2-year-olds in a center?" |
| "What if a child gets hurt?" | "How do I document a scraped knee?" |
| "Can parents be late?" | "What do I do if a parent is 30 minutes late?" |

## Quick action buttons

When you open a new chat, you will see quick action buttons:

- **Fire drill rules** — Quick fire safety info
- **CPR renewal** — Check your certification status
- **Staff ratios** — Get ratio requirements for your room
- **Inspection prep** — What to know before an inspection
- **What's due?** — See what's coming up

Click any button to ask that question instantly. After the AI answers, you may see follow-up suggestions at the bottom to keep the conversation going.

## Sharing an answer

Hover over the AI's answer and click the share icon:

- **Copy** — Paste it into a note or message
- **Print** — Print it to post in your classroom
- **Email** — Send it to yourself or your director

## Finding your past questions

Your previous chats show up in the list on the left side. Click any past conversation to see it again, or use the search box to find specific topics.

## When to ask your director instead

Use the AI for quick reference, but **talk to your director** for:

- Anything involving a specific child's situation
- Parent conflicts or complaints
- Unusual incidents
- Questions about your facility's specific policies
- Anything you are unsure about

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="The AI is wrong about something">
  AI can make mistakes. If an answer seems wrong, ask a follow-up to clarify, check with your director, and remember that your facility's policies take priority.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I can't access the chat">
  Make sure you are logged into ReadyRule. If you still can't access it, ask your director to check your account permissions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The answer is confusing">
  Ask a follow-up like "Can you explain that more simply?", "What does [term] mean?", or "Give me the short version."
</Accordion>
