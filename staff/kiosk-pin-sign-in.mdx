The staff kiosk is a shared-device sign-in page that your facility bookmarks on a tablet or computer near the entrance. Any staff member can clock in or out by selecting their name and entering their PIN.

## Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your name">
    On the kiosk screen, scroll through the staff list and tap your name. Each entry shows your role and current status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your PIN">
    Type your personal PIN on the keypad. If your PIN is verified, you proceed to the action screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an action">
    Select the action that matches what you are doing: **Sign In**, **Start Break**, **End Break**, or **Sign Out**. A confirmation message appears with the timestamp.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

The kiosk page runs at `/staff-kiosk/{facilityId}` and does not require a logged-in session. This makes it ideal for a shared tablet at your facility entrance.

After you complete an action, the kiosk shows a confirmation screen for 5 seconds, then automatically returns to the staff list for the next person.

### Two sign-in options

Your facility may offer two ways to check in:

| Method | URL | Best for |
|--------|-----|----------|
| **Kiosk (PIN)** | `/staff-kiosk/{facilityId}` | Shared tablet at the facility |
| **QR (session)** | `/staff-qr/{facilityId}` | Scanning a QR code on your personal phone |

The QR option uses your logged-in session, so no PIN is needed. The kiosk option always requires a PIN since multiple people share the device.

<Tip>If you do not have a PIN set up, ask your facility administrator to assign one. You cannot use the kiosk without a PIN.</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="PIN not working">
  Double-check that you are entering the correct PIN. If you have forgotten it, ask your administrator to reset it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Name not on the list">
  Only active staff members appear on the kiosk. If you do not see your name, confirm with your administrator that you have been added as staff.
</Accordion>
