When your facility director invites you to ReadyRule, you go through a quick setup to get your account ready.

<img src="/images/staff/getting-started-overview.png" alt="Staff getting started overview showing onboarding steps" />

## Staff onboarding

After your director adds you and you accept your invite, the onboarding flow walks you through three steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Welcome">
    Confirms your name and facility.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verification">
    Verifies your identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="All set">
    Shows what you can do with ReadyRule:

    | Feature | What it does |
    |---------|-------------|
    | **Compliance Status** | See your facility's live compliance score |
    | **Certification Reminders** | Get alerts before your certs expire |
    | **Daily Pulse** | Your daily checklist for compliance tasks |

    Click **Go to Dashboard** to start using ReadyRule.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your daily workflow

Once you are set up, here is what a typical day looks like:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the dashboard">
    Look for any alerts or action items when you arrive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your Daily Pulse checklist">
    Complete today's compliance tasks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log attendance">
    Record attendance as children arrive and depart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Respond to alerts">
    Handle late pickups or ratio concerns as they come up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Need help?

- Use the **AI chat** on the dashboard to ask questions about regulations or compliance
- Check with your director if you have questions about facility-specific policies
