title: "Attendance history"
description: "View your clock-in/out history and check your hours as a staff member."
---

ReadyRule keeps a log of every time you clock in, clock out, or take a break. You can view your attendance history for any past date to check your hours or confirm your schedule.

<img src="/images/staff/attendance-history-overview.png" alt="Staff attendance history showing daily events and net hours" />

## Quick start: check today's hours

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Attendance Log">
    Tap **Attendance Log** from the sidebar menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the Staff tab">
    At the top you will see two tabs: **Children** and **Staff**. Tap **Staff**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your name">
    Your events are grouped under your name and show each action in order:

    ```text
    Sarah Johnson (Lead Teacher)
      8:15 AM   Signed In
      12:00 PM  Started Break
      12:30 PM  Ended Break
      Still on site
      Hours so far: 6h 45m (in progress)
    ```

    If you have already clocked out, it shows your final net hours instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View a past date

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use the date picker">
    At the top of the Staff tab, tap the **date picker** and select the date you want to review. If you didn't work that day, your entry shows "No events."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your hours">
    For completed shifts, your net hours show the total time on site minus any breaks:

    ```text
    Sarah Johnson (Lead Teacher)
      8:00 AM   Signed In
      12:00 PM  Started Break
      12:30 PM  Ended Break
      5:00 PM   Signed Out
      Net hours: 8h 30m
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understanding your events

| What you see | What it means |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Signed In** | You clocked in at the facility |
| **Signed Out** | You clocked out for the day |
| **Started Break** | Your break began |
| **Ended Break** | You returned from break |
| **Net hours** | Total hours minus break time |
| **Hours (in progress)** | You haven't clocked out yet — updates live |

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="I don't see my name in the list">
  Your admin may not have added you as active staff yet. Let them know.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My hours look wrong">
  Check if a break was started but never ended, or if you forgot to clock out. Ask your admin to correct the record if needed — they can edit attendance entries.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I see last week's hours?">
  Yes. Use the date picker to go to any past date. Your records are kept for 3 years.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I don't see the Staff tab">
  Make sure you are on the **Attendance Log** page (not Attendance Reports). Look for the tabs at the top of the page.
</Accordion>

## Quick reference

| Task | How | Time |
|------|-----|------|
| Check today's hours | Attendance Log > Staff tab > find your name | 15 sec |
| Check a past date | Attendance Log > Staff tab > date picker > select date | 20 sec |
| See if you took breaks | Look for "Started Break" / "Ended Break" entries | 10 sec |
| Confirm net hours | Look at the "Net hours" line under your name | 5 sec |
