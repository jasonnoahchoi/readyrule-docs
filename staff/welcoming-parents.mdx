---
title: "Welcoming parents"
description: "Send welcome messages to parents so they know how to use QR sign-in at your facility."
---

When you add children to your facility roster, their authorized adults need to know that your facility now uses digital sign-in. ReadyRule lets you send welcome emails and text messages to all parents at once, explaining how QR sign-in works.

## Quick start: send welcome messages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your Child Roster">
    Navigate to **Attendance > Child Roster** from the sidebar. You will see an amber banner at the top if any parents haven't been welcomed yet:

    > **5 parents haven't been welcomed yet** — [Preview Message] [Send Welcome to All]
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview the message (optional)">
    Tap **Preview Message** to see exactly what parents will receive. A modal shows two tabs:
    - **Email** tab — The full email with QR instructions and your facility name
    - **SMS** tab — The text message (for parents without email)

    The preview also shows recipient counts: how many parents will get email vs. SMS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send welcome to all">
    Tap **Send Welcome to All**. You will see a loading spinner while messages are sent, then a confirmation: "Welcome sent to 5 parents."

    - Parents **with an email address** receive the welcome email
    - Parents **with only a phone number** receive an SMS instead
    - Parents **with neither** are skipped (add contact info in the roster first)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checking welcome status

After sending, each parent's welcome status appears as a badge next to their name in the roster.

| Badge | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Not sent** (gray) | Welcome hasn't been sent yet |
| **Sent (email)** (green) | Email delivered, with relative time (e.g., "3d ago") |
| **Sent (SMS)** (green) | Text message delivered |
| **Sent (both)** (green) | Both email and SMS sent |

Hover over any green badge to see the exact date and time.

## What parents receive

### Email

Parents get a branded email from ReadyRule with your facility's name, "How it works" steps (scan QR, enter code, tap to sign in), a note that no app download is needed, and your facility's contact info.

Subject line examples:
- "Sunshine Daycare has set up digital sign-in for Emma"
- "Sunshine Daycare has set up digital sign-in for Emma and family" (multiple children)

### Text message

Parents without email get a short SMS:

> "[Facility Name] now uses ReadyRule for sign-in/out. Just scan the QR code at drop-off — no app needed. Reply STOP to opt out."

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="The banner says 0 parents to welcome but I just added a child">
  The count refreshes every 60 seconds. Wait a moment or refresh the page.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A parent says they didn't get the welcome email">
  Check their email address in the roster for typos. Next to each parent's name, you will see resend buttons — an SMS icon to resend the text message and an email icon to resend the email. Each button is rate-limited to 3 sends per parent per 24 hours.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I updated a parent's phone number or email — do I need to resend?">
  Yes. When you change a parent's phone or email, their welcome status automatically resets to "Not sent" for that channel. Use the resend button to send to their updated contact.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I customize the welcome message?">
  Not yet. The message is standardized to ensure parents get clear, consistent instructions. Your facility name and child's name are automatically included.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Some messages show as skipped">
  Those parents don't have email or phone on file. Edit their contact info in the child roster, then resend.
</Accordion>
