Your profile page is where you manage your personal information, certifications, clearance status, and notification preferences. It is separate from facility-level settings, which only administrators can access.

<img src="/images/staff/profile-and-certs-overview.png" alt="Staff profile and certifications page" />

## Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Navigate to **Profile** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your details">
    Edit your first name, last name, and phone number. Your email is read-only — contact support to change it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add certifications">
    Scroll to **My Certifications** and click **Add Certification**. Select the certification type, enter the expiration date, and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Account details

Your profile shows your name, email, and phone number. You can update your name and phone directly. Changes save immediately after you click **Save**.

## Clearances

The **My Clearances** section shows the status of your background checks:

- **Criminal Record Clearance** — current status of your criminal background check
- **Child Abuse Index (CACI)** — current status of your CACI check

Clearance statuses are managed by your administrator. Contact them if a status needs updating.

## Certifications

You can add, edit, and delete your own certifications. Each certification shows:

- Certification type (CPR/First Aid, Mandated Reporter, etc.)
- Expiration date
- Status badge: **Current**, **Expiring soon** (within 30 days), or **Expired**

ReadyRule tracks expiration dates and alerts both you and your administrator when certifications are approaching expiry.

## Notification preferences

At the bottom of the profile page, configure how you receive notifications — email, SMS, in-app, or a combination. Set quiet hours to avoid alerts outside of work time.

<Tip>Keep your certifications up to date in the system. Expiring or expired certifications affect your facility's compliance score and generate alerts for your administrator.</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="Cannot see My Certifications">
  The certifications section requires an active staff record. If you do not see it, your administrator may not have linked your account as staff yet.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Cannot change my email">
  Email changes require support assistance for security reasons. Contact the ReadyRule support team to update your email address.
</Accordion>
