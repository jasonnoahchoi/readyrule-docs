When your administrator invites you to ReadyRule, you go through a short onboarding flow to set up your profile and enter your compliance records. The process takes about 5 minutes.

<img src="/images/staff/getting-started-overview.png" alt="Staff onboarding welcome screen" />

## Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept the invitation">
    Click the invite link from your email. Create your account or log in with your existing credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Welcome screen">
    You see a welcome message with your facility's name and your assigned role. Review the information and continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Background checks">
    Enter the status of your Criminal Record Clearance and Child Abuse Index (CACI) checks. If you have not completed these yet, you can skip and update later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Certifications">
    Add your certifications — CPR/First Aid, Mandated Reporter, and any others you hold. Enter expiration dates for each. You can skip this step and add them from your profile later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Onboarding steps

The onboarding flow has three steps, tracked by a progress indicator at the top:

| Step | What you do |
|------|-------------|
| **Welcome** | Confirm your facility and role |
| **Background Check** | Enter your clearance statuses |
| **Certifications** | Add CPR, First Aid, Mandated Reporter, and other certs |

You can skip the background check and certification steps if you do not have the information handy. Your administrator will see that these are incomplete and may follow up.

## After onboarding

Once you complete onboarding, you land on the main app where you can:

- **Check in and out** using the kiosk or QR code
- **View your profile** to update certifications and preferences
- **Use AI chat** to ask compliance questions
- **Browse violations** relevant to your facility

<Tip>Complete all onboarding steps on day one if possible. Having your background checks and certifications in the system from the start prevents compliance gaps.</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="Invite link expired">
  Ask your administrator to resend the invitation. Invite links have an expiration window for security.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Wrong facility shown">
  If the welcome screen shows a different facility, contact your administrator. They may need to update your staff record.
</Accordion>
