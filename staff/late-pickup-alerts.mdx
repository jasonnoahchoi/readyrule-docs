---
title: "Late pickup alerts"
description: "Monitor and respond to children still present near closing time."
---

Late Pickup Alerts help you stay on top of children who haven't been picked up as your facility approaches closing time. Alerts appear automatically on your dashboard — no setup required.

## Alert states

### Warning (orange)
Appears **30 minutes before closing time**. Children are still present and closing is approaching.

### Critical (red)
Appears **after closing time has passed**. Immediate parent contact is recommended.

## Using alerts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your dashboard">
    Alerts appear automatically when conditions are met. You'll see the number of children still present and time remaining.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review children listed">
    Each child card shows the child's name, who signed them in, and a **Call** button linking to the parent's phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact parents">
    Tap the **Call** button to open your phone's dialer with the parent's number pre-filled. If a phone number is missing, the button appears disabled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Alert timing

| Window | When | What it means |
|--------|------|---------------|
| **Warning** | 30 min before close | Prepare for end-of-day, consider reminding parents |
| **Critical** | After closing time | Children still present, contact parents immediately |

Alerts refresh every 60 seconds. As children are signed out, they're removed from the alert list.

<Tip>During the 30-minute window before closing, check your dashboard every 5-10 minutes. Use warning alerts as a prompt to prepare rather than waiting for critical alerts.</Tip>

<Accordion title="Alert not appearing?">
Check that your facility's closing time is configured in **Settings**, children are currently signed in, and the current time is within 30 minutes of closing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Call button not working?">
Verify that the parent's phone number is on file in the correct format. Update contact information in the Child Roster if needed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I customize the warning threshold?">
The 30-minute warning window is currently fixed. Your admin can adjust the late pickup warning threshold in **Settings** to control when children are highlighted on the dashboard.
</Accordion>
