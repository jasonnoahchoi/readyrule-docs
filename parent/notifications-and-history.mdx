ReadyRule lets you see your child's sign-in and sign-out times, browse 30 days of attendance history, get instant notifications when your child is picked up or dropped off, and control when and how you get notified.

## Viewing today's activity

When you open the ReadyRule app on your phone, you will see your child's name and a timeline below it showing today's events.

```text
Emma (Preschool Room)

9:45 AM  Signed in by You
12:30 PM Signed out by John (Parent)
3:15 PM  Signed in by Sarah (Teacher)
```

Each entry shows the time, whether your child was signed in or out, and who did the signing.

## Viewing 30-day history

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your child's card">
    On the Family app home screen, find your child's card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the history">
    Scroll down past today's activity and tap **"View Full History"** or **"History"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse past days">
    Each day shows the total time at the facility and a sign-in/out timeline:

    ```text
    March 3, 2026
    Total time: 4 hours 45 minutes

    9:30 AM  Signed in by You
    2:15 PM  Signed out by John (Parent)
    ```

    Tap any date to see the full timeline. Scroll up or down for older or more recent days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Enabling push notifications

Get notified right away when your child is signed in or out.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look for the notification banner">
    When you first open the app, look for a banner that says **"Stay connected with push notifications"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Enable">
    Tap **"Enable"** or **"Turn On Notifications"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow notifications">
    Your phone will ask for permission. Tap **"Allow"** in the permission dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What notifications look like

**When your child is signed in:**
> Emma signed in — Emma was signed in at Sunshine Preschool at 9:45 AM

**When your child is picked up:**
> Emma picked up — Emma was picked up from Sunshine Preschool at 2:30 PM by John

### If you don't see the banner

**On iPhone:** Go to Settings > Notifications > ReadyRule > Make sure "Allow Notifications" is on.

**On Android:** Go to Settings > Apps > ReadyRule > Notifications > Make sure notifications are enabled.

## Managing your notification preferences

Open the Family app, tap the **Settings** icon (gear icon) or **Preferences** in the menu.

### Quiet hours

Set a time window where you don't want to receive notifications. For example, set 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM to skip alerts during sleep time. The system respects overnight windows that cross midnight.

### Push notifications

Toggle push notifications on or off. If you have quiet hours set, they still apply even when push is enabled.

### Text message (SMS) and email

Toggle text message and email notifications on or off independently. If you turn on SMS, you may see a consent checkbox.

### Late pickup alerts

Toggle late pickup alerts on or off to receive a notification if your child is picked up later than usual.

### Saving your changes

After changing any settings, scroll to the bottom and tap **Save** or **Done**. You will see a confirmation message.

## Updating your phone number or email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to contact settings">
    Open the Family app and go to **Settings** > **Preferences** > **Contact Information**. Your current phone number and email are masked for privacy (e.g., `***-***-4567`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Change Phone or Change Email">
    Tap the change link next to the contact you want to update.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your new contact info">
    Enter the new phone number or email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify with a code">
    Tap **"Send Verification Code"**. You will receive a 6-digit code by text (for phone) or email. The code is valid for 5 minutes. Enter the code and tap **"Verify"**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>For security, you can only change your contact info 3 times per month. Your old phone number still works for sign-in until you change it.</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="I'm not getting notifications">
  1. Check quiet hours — notifications won't come during that time
  2. Check push notifications setting — make sure it is toggled on
  3. Check phone permissions — go to your phone's settings and make sure ReadyRule has notification permission
  4. Check your connection — notifications need a working internet connection
  5. Try refreshing — close the app completely and reopen it
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My quiet hours don't seem to be working">
  Quiet hours respect your timezone. If they seem off, adjust the start and end times and double-check your timezone setting in preferences.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The verification code isn't working">
  1. Make sure you are entering exactly 6 digits (no spaces or dashes)
  2. Check that the code hasn't expired (5-minute window)
  3. If expired, request a new code by tapping "Resend"
  4. Make sure the code was received on the right channel (SMS for phone, email for email)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I can't change my contact info">
  You might have reached the rate limit (3 changes per 30 days). Wait a few days and try again, or contact support at support@readyrule.com.
</Accordion>

## Privacy and security

- Your contact info is masked — you will never see anyone else's full phone or email in the app
- Verification codes expire in 5 minutes and are used only once
- Push notifications are encrypted
- Only linked parents can see activity for their children
