Your child's facility uses ReadyRule for digital sign-in and sign-out. Instead of paper sign-in sheets, you scan a QR code at the entrance and verify your identity with a one-time code sent to your phone or email.

## Quick start: your first sign-in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    When you arrive at the facility, you will see a QR code posted at the entrance. Scan it with your phone's camera. You will see a welcome screen with your facility's name.
    <img src="/images/parent/qr-code-sign-in-landing.png" alt="QR code sign-in welcome screen" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your verification method">
    You will be asked how you'd like to verify your identity:
    - **Phone number** — "We'll text you a code"
    - **Email address** — "We'll email you a code"

    Tap your preferred option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your code">
    Check your phone (text) or email inbox for a 6-digit code. Enter it on screen. You will see a confirmation screen with your children's names.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in your children">
    Tap each child's name to sign them in. Your session lasts 30 days, so you won't need to enter a code again for a month.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Changing your preferences

After signing in, you can customize your experience. Tap the **settings gear icon** on the sign-in screen after verifying your identity.

### Sign-in method

Choose whether you verify with **Phone** or **Email** each time you sign in. Your choice is remembered for next time.

### Notifications

Control how the facility reaches you for alerts:

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Late pickup alerts** | Get notified if pickup is running late |
| **How to receive alerts** | Choose: Email only, Text only, or Both |

If you enable text messages, you will see a consent notice. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to unsubscribe.

### Quiet hours

Set times when you don't want to receive notifications:

- **Start time** — When quiet hours begin (e.g., 9:00 PM)
- **End time** — When quiet hours end (e.g., 7:00 AM)

During quiet hours, the facility won't send you any automated alerts. Urgent safety notifications may still come through.

### Save your preferences

Tap the **Save preferences** button at the bottom. You will see a confirmation that your settings were saved.

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="I'm getting texts but I don't want them">
  Reply **STOP** to any ReadyRule text message, or go to your Preferences and turn off "Text message" under notification delivery.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I changed my phone number — how do I update it?">
  Contact your facility directly. Staff can update your phone number in the system. Your next sign-in will use the new number.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My session expired and I have to enter a code again">
  Sessions last 30 days for security. Just enter a new code — it takes 10 seconds.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I have children at multiple facilities">
  Each facility has its own QR code and sign-in. Your preferences are saved separately per facility.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Authorization expired — what does that mean?">
  Your pickup authorization may have had an end date set by the parent or facility. Contact the primary parent to renew your authorization.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I don't see the welcome screen when scanning">
  Make sure you are scanning the QR code for your specific facility. If you have signed in before, you will go straight to the sign-in screen instead.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I didn't receive a code">
  Check your spam folder (for email) or wait 30 seconds and request a new code. Make sure the phone number or email on file with the facility is correct.
</Accordion>

## Quick reference

| Task | How | Time |
|------|-----|------|
| Sign in your child | Scan QR > Enter code > Tap child's name | 30 sec |
| Change sign-in method | Preferences > Sign-In Method > Phone or Email | 15 sec |
| Set quiet hours | Preferences > Quiet Hours > Set times > Save | 30 sec |
| Turn off text alerts | Preferences > Notifications > Change delivery method | 15 sec |
| Sign out your child | Scan QR > Tap child's name > Confirm sign-out | 20 sec |

## Privacy

- Your phone number and email are only used for sign-in codes and facility alerts
- No app download or account creation required
- Your data is only shared with your child's facility
- You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP
