The family dashboard is your mobile-first hub for managing your children's attendance at their facility. It shows real-time sign-in status, lets you sign children in and out, and gives you access to attendance history and guardian management.

<img src="/images/parent/sign-in-preferences-landing.png" alt="Family dashboard on desktop" />

## Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the family dashboard">
    Visit the link your facility shared (e.g., `readyrule.com/family/013423682`). If you have a custom URL, it may look like `readyrule.com/family/sunshine-daycare`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in or verify your phone">
    First-time users enter their phone number and verify with a one-time code. Returning users are recognized automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View your children">
    Your linked children appear with their current sign-in status. Tap a child to sign them in or out.
    <img src="/images/parent/sign-in-preferences-landing-mobile.png" alt="Family dashboard on mobile" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you see

The dashboard displays:

- **Facility name and logo** at the top
- **Your children** with real-time status (signed in, signed out)
- **Sign-in and sign-out buttons** with large touch targets (optimized for mobile)
- **Pull-to-refresh** — swipe down to update statuses
- **Guardian management** — view and manage authorized adults linked to your children
- **Attendance history** — review past sign-in and sign-out records

## Signing children in and out

Tap a child's card to toggle their attendance status. The dashboard confirms the action with a visual indicator and timestamp. Status updates are instant and visible to the facility administrator.

## Guardian management

From the dashboard, you can view the list of authorized guardians linked to your children. This shows who has permission to pick up each child. Contact the facility administrator to add or remove guardians.

<Tip>Add the family dashboard to your phone's home screen for app-like quick access. ReadyRule supports installation as a Progressive Web App (PWA).</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="Facility not found">
  Double-check the link or scan the QR code again. The facility number in the URL must match an active ReadyRule facility.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Children not showing up">
  Your phone number must match what the facility has on file for you as an authorized adult. Contact the facility to verify your contact information.
</Accordion>
